Connector ID — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Connector ID ("the app") is published by aired.com. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

Photos you capture

When you take or select a photo to identify a connector, the image is sent over an encrypted connection to our identification service at aired.com, processed to return a connector type and confidence, and the result is shown to you in the app. Photos are used only to provide the identification feature. We do not sell them, use them for advertising, or associate them with your identity.

Connector requests

If you use the "Request a connector type" feature, the app opens your device's email app with a pre-filled message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email yourself, and the content is whatever you write plus the app version and platform.

Information stored on your device

The app stores app settings and a device token locally on your device (in standard app storage and the system keychain) so it can communicate with our service. This information stays on your device and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

Permissions

The app requests camera and photo library access solely to capture or select connector images for identification. It does not record audio.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Connector ID is a professional utility and is not directed to children.

Data removal

To request deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, contact us at the address below.
